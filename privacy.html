Privacy Policy — AssembleWise

Effective: October 13, 2026 · Applies to assemblewise.com and the AssembleWise app (Phase 1 — free, no ads, no payments).

What AssembleWise is

AssembleWise turns furniture-assembly manuals into free step-by-step guided builds with voice narration in English, Spanish and French.

What we collect

  • No accounts. Phase 1 has no sign-up, no login, and we do not ask for your name, address, or payment information.
  • On-device progress only. Your build progress and language choice are saved in your own browser/device storage (localStorage) so you can resume where you left off. That data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.
  • No ads, no ad trackers. Monetization is OFF in Phase 1: no advertising SDKs, no sale of personal information, no cross-app tracking.
  • Basic hosting logs. Our hosting provider may record standard technical logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) for security and operation of the site. We do not use these logs to build advertising profiles.
  • Email you send us. If you contact us, we keep your message and email address only to reply and to improve the guides.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with marketers.
  • We do not collect payment data (there is nothing to buy in Phase 1).
  • AssembleWise is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

  • Clear your browser/device storage at any time to erase saved progress and preferences.
  • Email us to ask what correspondence we hold from you, or to have it deleted.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, ads, or paid features, this policy will be updated and dated before those features launch, and the app-store listings will be updated to match.
